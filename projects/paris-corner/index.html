05 Case study — E-commerce · Luxury D2C

Paris Corner UAE

A premium Shopify storefront for a UAE fragrance house carrying nine sub-brands — where the product has to carry the page and the interface has to get out of its way.

Built the storefront end to end in Liquid — product presentation, collection browsing and the visual language that keeps a nine-brand catalogue feeling like one house.

01 Problem

Nine sub-brands, hundreds of SKUs and heavy combo merchandising were all competing for the same attention. Shoppers browsing by scent and shoppers browsing by brand kept hitting dead ends.

Every merchandising change was a developer ticket, which meant the storefront moved slower than the marketing calendar.

02 Approach
  • Let the photography lead: dark, quiet surfaces and thin gold rules instead of decorative chrome.
  • Made brand and scent both first-class browsing paths rather than nesting one inside the other.
  • Built the theme as composable Liquid sections, so merchandising can be rearranged without touching code.
  • Kept price, size and combo savings on one predictable line so comparison takes no effort.
03 Solution

A restrained luxury storefront built entirely in Liquid on Shopify: a centred wordmark identity, editorial hero banners, brand and collection landing surfaces, and combo merchandising blocks.

The product card stays readable whether it holds a single bottle or a bundle, which is what lets one grid serve the whole catalogue.

04 Impact
  • Nine sub-brands read as one house rather than nine competing storefronts.
  • Shoppers move brand → scent → bundle without leaving the browsing flow.
  • Merchandising changes ship through Shopify sections rather than developer tickets.
