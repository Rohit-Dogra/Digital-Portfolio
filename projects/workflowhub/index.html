03 Case study — SaaS · Productivity · Workflow

WorkflowHub

An internal operations platform where tasks, tickets and daily account checks live in one workspace — with separate admin, manager and employee portals.

Built the board, task and role-based portal experience, including the Zoho Desk ticket sync surface.

01 Problem

Marketplace operations ran on chat threads, spreadsheets and a separate helpdesk. Nobody could answer "who owns this and where is it" without asking three people.

Recurring per-account checks lived in individual habits rather than anywhere a manager could see, so coverage depended on who was at their desk.

02 Approach
  • Modelled the real workflow first — task, owner, account, state — and designed around that instead of around a generic to-do list.
  • Gave each role only the surface it needs: admins configure, managers assign, employees execute.
  • Offered board, list and calendar views of the same data rather than forcing one mental model on everyone.
  • Brought support tickets into the same queue as internal work, so nothing lives in two systems at once.
03 Solution

A workspace with kanban, list and calendar views, two-way Zoho Desk ticket sync, per-account daily checklists, file management with versioning, and dashboards for velocity and workload.

Authentication runs on JWT with httpOnly cookies, CSRF protection and encrypted sessions, with granular permissions behind each portal.

04 Impact
  • Ownership and status are visible without a status meeting.
  • Support tickets and internal work sit in one queue instead of two systems.
  • Recurring account checks became a repeatable checklist rather than tribal knowledge.
