04 Case study — Agency · Digital Growth

CoTask

The public face of a technology and digital-growth agency — marketplace management, performance marketing and e-commerce development, all made legible to a first-time visitor.

Designed the service architecture and conversion-oriented layouts, and built the responsive frontend.

01 Problem

The agency does a lot — seven marketplaces, five marketing channels, Shopify builds, vendor EDI account work. Presented as a flat list, that breadth read as unfocused rather than capable.

Credentials and partnerships were scattered as badges instead of building a case.

02 Approach
  • Grouped services by the outcome a client wants, not by the internal team that delivers them.
  • Gave credentials and partnerships a dedicated proof layer where they reinforce each other.
  • Kept one low-friction consultation path, repeated at consistent intervals down the page.
  • Designed in Figma first, then built the same components in code so nothing drifted between file and production.
03 Solution

A modern, conversion-oriented marketing site: outcome-grouped service tiles, marketplace coverage, a credentials strip, a blog surface, and a single recurring enquiry action.

Built responsive-first on a shared component set so new service pages inherit the system.

04 Impact
  • A broad service range now reads as focused expertise rather than a menu.
  • Visitors can self-identify their need before ever speaking to sales.
  • New service pages drop into an existing pattern instead of needing bespoke design.
