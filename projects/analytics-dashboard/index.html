02 Case study — SaaS · Analytics · Dashboard

Analytics Dashboard

A unified reporting surface for Amazon sellers and agencies — sales, inventory, returns and advertising pulled from a dozen connected platforms into one readable view.

Designed the KPI, trend and drill-down patterns that make multi-marketplace data legible at a glance.

01 Problem

Sellers were reading their own business across a dozen disconnected consoles — Seller Central, Vendor Central, Amazon Advertising, Google Ads, Noon and more. Every question about performance began with an export and ended in a spreadsheet.

By the time a number was assembled by hand it was already stale, so decisions were being made on last week's picture.

02 Approach
  • Started from the questions, not the data: what does an operator need to know in the first five seconds of opening this?
  • Built a strict hierarchy — headline KPIs, then trend, then breakdown, then row-level detail — so every module reads the same way and the eye learns the pattern once.
  • Chose chart types by the shape of the question: trend to line, comparison to bar, composition to stacked. Never decoration.
  • Designed the loading and empty states first, because a data product spends real time in both.
  • Used tabular figures and locale-aware formatting so columns of numbers stay aligned and scannable.
03 Solution

A modular dashboard covering revenue analytics, inventory intelligence, returns analysis and advertising performance, with role-based workspaces separating multi-brand operators from agency accounts.

Reserved chart space and consistent number formatting keep the layout stable as data streams in, so nothing jumps while you're reading it.

04 Impact
  • Performance questions that used to mean cross-referencing several consoles now resolve on one screen.
  • The module pattern absorbs new data sources without a redesign each time one is connected.
  • Reporting shifted from manual exports to a live, shareable surface.
